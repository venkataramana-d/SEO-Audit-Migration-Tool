What each flag means (where it comes from & why it matters)

BrokenWhere: a link on this page points to a URL that returned 404/5xx. Why it matters: broken links waste crawl budget, leak link equity into dead ends, and hurt user experience. Fix: update or remove the link, or restore the target.
Internal nofollowWhere: a link to your own site carries rel="nofollow". Why it matters: it blocks ranking value from flowing between your own pages. Fix: remove nofollow from internal links.
External dofollowWhere: an outbound link to another domain with no rel="nofollow/sponsored/ugc". Why it matters: it passes your ranking value out to that site. Fix: add rel="nofollow" unless you deliberately vouch for it.
Unsafe new-tabWhere: a link with target="_blank" but no rel="noopener". Why it matters: the opened page can access your window object (security & performance risk). Fix: add rel="noopener".
